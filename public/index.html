Our story

A future, grown from the ground up.

Their story began with a choice: to build a future close to home. Binu and Sincy believed the land they called their own could become the foundation for a successful life. While others might look abroad for opportunity, they looked to farming—and to animal husbandry—as their way forward.

They began on a small scale. With dedication, hard work, and careful management, their pig-farming activities grew into a successful venture. Bethel Organic Farm carries that belief forward: that self-reliance can take root, and a future can be built right here in our own homeland.

Before you come

A few things to know.

Plan ahead and contact the farm to arrange the right experience for your visit.

How do I book?

All visits are by advance booking. Contact the farm by phone, WhatsApp, or email. The advance notice needed depends on the activity or stay.

What can I do at the farm?

Choose a day visit, room stay, camping, farm tour, trekking or nature walk, riverside picnic, grill or BBQ, restaurant meal, or kids’ activities. Timing and availability depend on the experience.

Are meals included?

All meals are included with an overnight stay. Day visitors can order Kerala meals, with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options. Ask for the current menu and prices.

Can guests swim at the riverside?

No. Riverside visits are for enjoying the setting; swimming is not available.

Where is Bethel Organic Farm?

Arppathanath House, Nochupully P.O., Mundur, Puthuppariyaram Village, Palakkad District, Kerala 678592. The map in the contact section shows the exact location and directions.
